# A five-minute Forma demo

1. Open Overview. Change the reporting period and toggle the comparison. Explain that metrics follow local order records and traffic is fictional.
2. Open Products. Search for “notebook”, edit its stock count, and save. The updated count appears in the catalog and the overview's low-stock list.
3. Create a demo order using a fictional customer. Choose products and quantities, set shipping and discount, and save. Check that inventory decreases and the order appears in history.
4. Change that order to Cancelled. Its reserved units return to inventory. Payment status is a separate editable local simulation.
5. Open Merchandising. Test WELCOME10 against different cart subtotals, then change the featured collection.
6. Review “The autumn edit” in Publishing, preview it, and publish locally. The queue updates.
7. Open Activity & history. Inspect a product or content change, then restore its previous values as a new revision.
8. Press Ctrl/Cmd+K and jump directly to a product or demo customer. Show the dark theme and mobile navigation.
9. In Settings, rename the business, choose an accent, show local team profiles, then export or reset the demo for the next presentation.

Suggested portfolio caption: “Forma — an independently built operations dashboard for business and commerce, with transactional local persistence, reusable domain models, accessible editing workflows, and responsive light/dark interfaces. All demonstration data is fictional.”
